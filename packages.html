How It Works

Three levels. One professional standard.

We evaluate the condition of the existing coating and painted surfaces, then explain which level of preparation makes sense. We explain what is included in each option so you can choose the level of preparation that best fits the condition of your home and your goals. Final estimates are based on home size, condition, access, number of colors and project scope.

Option 1 • Great Value

Essential

For exteriors in generally good condition that need professional basic preparation and a fresh protective finish.

  • Pressure wash
  • Basic scraping, sanding & paint preparation
  • Basic caulking and painting-related fillers as needed
  • Spot prime bare or prepared areas as needed
  • Quality exterior paint
  • Standard 2-coat coverage
  • Up to 2 colors
  • 2-year workmanship warranty
